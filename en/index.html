The place

Small, bright,
and cool when it matters.

Cafetería zoe sits on Rambla de Sant Jordi in the centre of Vilanova i la Geltrú. It is a small room that clearly gets looked after: well lit, with music kept low and air conditioning running — which around here in summer is not a small thing.

Zoe works the counter and remembers how you take your coffee. Ask for it properly hot and it arrives properly hot; ask for it warm and that is what you get. It is the kind of place you go back to out of habit rather than novelty.
